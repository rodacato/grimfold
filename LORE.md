# Lore — the bay

The truth under Grimfold's first Lugar, for whoever writes a diary, a Carta, a place or the next
volume. **Spoilers: this is the whole mystery.** The game never shows any of it whole; it shows
fragments (§8). Lines the player reads are es-MX and marked `»`; everything else is author truth and
**never goes into player text as written**.

The spine is in [docs/WORLDS.md](docs/WORLDS.md); this file is what lies under it. Tone: hinted,
never named. No Lovecraft names or quotes; public-domain texts give tone only (§10).

## 1. What sleeps

Under the bay sleeps something that was there before the water. It does not want anything and does
not know the town exists. While it sleeps it dreams by **copying what falls in**: rings, teeth, a
bell, a vote. The copies swim. If it woke, nothing would rise: it would only **look**, and whatever
it looks at joins the dream. A sleep like that needs someone awake beside it. The keepers kept watch
with the light; when the town voted the light off, the keeper went down to keep watch from inside.

**What it is not:** a god, a cult, a tentacled giant, hungry, angry, from the stars, a punishment or
the father's ghost. It is never seen whole (the closest is an eyelid), never named beyond
» *lo que duerme*, and cannot be fished, fought or killed.

## 2. The laws of the sea

Use them to decide any new creature, place or event. An idea that breaks one belongs to another world.

| # | Law | Decides |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | **The dark erases distance.** In the dark, the bottom and the net are the same place | Why the fish come on their own; why stealing the Luz brings things closer |
| L2 | **Light makes an *arriba*.** It is not for ships; it marks where up is | The lighthouse, the anguilas, the linterna |
| L3 | **What falls in is learned.** Every creature is a copy of something the town gave the water | Each new creature needs an offering: an object, a habit, a gesture |
| L4 | **The sea keeps what a thing was for, not who it was** | The Farolero keeps the light, not the man; the motor still wants to go far |
| L5 | **To be seen is to be changed.** Waking is an eye opening, not a body rising | The Marcas; answering a voice; copies that close their eyes |
| L6 | **Every copy comes out a little crooked**: one ring short, one tooth bent, a song off key | How the town recognizes a copy; every new creature carries one error |
| L7 | **It has happened before.** The bay has had other towns and other keepers | The town under the lake, the 1911 sturgeon |

**Cordura and the Marcas** follow L5: the Arponera is being looked at, and the look gives before it
takes. *Ojos de bruma*: she sees as it sees, without distance (the Vida of what rises). *Brazo de
ventosas*: she reaches as it reaches (+1 damage). At 0 she is learned (L3) and kept for what she is
for (L4), which is her father's job.

## 3. The night of the vote

March. The nets had come up empty for a year and the debt was due. The keeper came to the
cooperativa to say what the dark lets through; Chela, who holds every key on the dock, closed the
door, and he stayed outside, talking through the wood. Don Tomás wrote down what he said and did not
raise his hand, because the one who counts does not vote. Eleven hands went up. Nobody switched the
light off: the keeper did it himself that night. He asked five people to his last fire on the
island; only Marta came, and she left her rebozo on a chair when he walked into the water. The next
morning the fish were at the dock, and the church bell stopped ringing. A week later Chela tore the
page out of the acta. Don Tomás blamed a rat.

**Where he is.** The man went down and stays below; what climbed back up to the tower is his watch,
the Farolero. Both endings speak of him *abajo*; the tower holds only his job. The town says *farero*
for the man and never for the Farolero.

## 4. The Arponera

The keeper's daughter, grown: red raincoat, his harpoon. She goes out because **she hears something
calling**, and because she wants to know what the light is in a lighthouse that should not be lit.
Under that, she wants to know whether he left her. She does not know that he warned the town
through a closed door, that the light she steals is his watch, that Marta's daughter may have been
her sister, or that each time she falls in the sea learns her a little: the Reina de los percebes
wears her face, unfinished.

## 5. The town

Every townsperson tells the truth in pieces small enough to be a lie.

| Who | Wants | Hides | Bends the truth by | Paid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nico** (9) | To see the keeper again | That he waved at the tower once and something waved back | Never lying: nobody believes a child. The bell is his mother's, so she can hear where he is | Nothing yet; he did not vote |
| **don Tomás** | A complete register; to understand rather than be forgiven | That he wrote the keeper's words and kept quiet, and knows the rat has keys | Recording facts, never meanings | No hand, no ring. The sea writes in his book now; the sifonóforo's empty chamber waits for him |
| **Chela** | Forgiveness without confessing | The door, and the page (she has it) | Small true facts: » *"Yo no lo apagué"* is true; in » *"Yo solo cerré la puerta"*, the *solo* is the lie | Nothing, so far. That is her terror |
| **Marta** | Her daughter back; failing that, him | That she raised her hand **for** the dark, because in the dark things come back on their own; that she went to the fire | Answering about the jar so she does not answer the rest | She got what she asked for: things come back, crooked (L6) |
| **Ruelas padre** | To fish someone out, since he could not fish his son | Nothing; he is the honest one, and he raised his hand | Working instead of talking | His son, the launch's motor, and his wedding ring, which he put on the boy for luck |

**Absent, never on screen:** the keeper, who chose the watch over his daughter, knowing it; Marta's
daughter, taken by the lake years before the vote, with the keeper's eyes; the Ruelas son, who went
out far the week after the vote to prove the town did not need the dark. The launch came back alone,
dinner on the table.

**The eleven hands.** Ten of them paid one thing each in the weeks after; Chela has not, yet. The
cangrejo carries wedding rings, and the Ruelas ones are not among them: one went down on the boy's
finger. No text ever says how many rings there are. **What the town gained:** full nets without going
far, the debt paid. **What it hides:** it knew the price.

## 6. Revelation by Capítulo

Each step adds one fact and never re-explains the last (N7). On paper only the Capítulo lines reach
the player, so no truth may live only in a diary.

| Step | Carries in | Learns | Leaves with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cap. 1 · Nico | Is dad alive? What is that light? | Someone in the tower wears his cap | Is that him? Why would he chase me? |
| Cap. 2 · don Tomás | Why is the lighthouse off? | The town voted: eleven hands, Chela's too. A page is missing | What was on the page? |
| Cap. 3 · Chela | What did dad do? | The page, in Tomás's hand: he spoke through the door, and went down on purpose | Did he leave me? Relight it, or go to him? |
| Prendes el faro | Can I put it back? | It sleeps; he stays below; the town must fish far again | Someone has to keep the watch after him |
| Bajas con él | Can I have him back? | He was waiting; the fish keep coming | What does a town become with nobody on watch? |

## 7. What each thing really is

**The Mazo.** Every creature is a copy of something the town gave the water (L3), with one error (L6).

| Creature | Underneath |
|---|---|
| anguila | Copies of what used to follow the light up; the wick is a lamp someone threw in |
| cangrejo | The sea's bookkeeper: it keeps what bound people, and counts. Two registers, its and Tomás's |
| globo | A copy made of a silence: the church bell that stopped in March. It swells at a sound that no longer exists |
| bagre | An opening, not a fish: what breathes through its held-open mouth is the sleeper's breath |
| raya | » *La Cara de Abajo*. The one copy that knows L5 and closes its eyes out of courtesy |
| sinojos | L1 alive: with no distance to see across, the eyes were never copied. The sea's first draft of a face |
| ajolote | A keeper's larva from the lake town; its black globes are lamps not yet lit. It foreshadows the Farolero |
| esturión | The oldest copy still swimming: 1911 and today are the same fish |
| lucio | The custom, returned: baby teeth thrown in the lake were offerings |
| medusa | The church bell, learned: below, it still rings at supper time |
| linterna | A light that lost its post when the keeper's went out, hence the shame |
| pez-hacha | The music under the dock; it stops when a lamp is lit |
| trípode | The vote, learned: raised hands, fingers bent backward, counted from below |
| sirena | Off key on purpose, as mercy: whoever hears it right is seen and stays |
| tiburón | Keeps what the Ruelas motor was for: going far |
| calamar | Anchors are tethers to the sleeper; lift one and you share its dream for a night |
| reina | The Arponera, being learned; her face is worn smooth because it is not finished. Art: a scrap of red raincoat in the barnacles (pending) |
| víbora | How the sea looks from inside: the face is in the throat. Writing it down is being seen too |
| sifonóforo | The song before the vote, in seven unfinished faces; the empty chamber waits for the one who only counted |
| duerme | Not the sleeper: its eyelid. The **ídolo** it gives is the sea's copy of the sleeper, faceless, because the sea does not know what it looks like either |
| gaviota | The only copy learned from above: the smile the town wore when the fish started arriving |
| mascarón | A copy of a copy: the town carved the keeper's face to watch the sea, and things made to watch wake up |
| **Farolero** | The keeper's watch, kept: the sea kept his job and let the man go. Four eyes, two on the town and two below. He chases you because you are taking the watch |

**Kept out:** *inquilino* (it breaks L4 and wears a body, the line §10 draws against *Annihilation*),
*ausencia* (the shape of the eye opening a crack: Capítulo 3 or ending art at most, never a Carta),
*nudo* and *desove* (desove's worlds-as-eggs would close the mystery).

The eight candidate locations are other edges of the same sleep, for later volumes; `pueblo-faro`
(not used in the game) loses its tentacles at its next redraw. **marta-mayor** is held for a

## 8. Fragments

Each truth is reachable from **about three fragments in different media** (diary, item, event,
place, chapter, art), and **none states it whole**. Every speaker has a bias: Chela lies by
omission, don Tomás records without understanding, Nico sees, Marta mourns, Ruelas works. If a
single text explains a law, cut it in two. Portraits are still being redrawn, so their details are
fragments to come.

| Truth | Carried by |
|---|---|
| Chela closed the door | anguila diary · Cap. 3 » *"Yo cerré la puerta"* · her key ring (portrait, pending) |
| Chela took the page | e2 (the archive's rat) · Cap. 2 » *"Falta una página"* · Tomás's torn book (portrait, pending) |
| The keeper switched it off himself | anguila » *"Yo no lo apagué"* · linterna » *"La última noche subió solo a la torre"* · the lamp room's empty chair (art) |
| Marta went to the fire | the rebozo (fogata art and text) · e4 » *"yo ya no tengo rebozo"* · her rebozo (portrait, pending) |
| Marta's daughter had his eyes | e9 » *"No preguntes de quién"* · lucio » *"Para los ojos no hay costumbre"* |
| The Ruelas son | cangrejo » *"Faltan los de los Ruelas"* · tiburón · naufragio-ruelas |
| The sea learns her | reina diary · evento nombre · the Marcas |
| It has happened before | esturión diary (1911) · ajolote diary · lago-campanario |

**Held, not in the game yet:** the register line » *"1911: se apagó el faro viejo. Mismo mes."*
(with the esturión diary it states L7 almost whole); the marta-mayor epilogue; a pass that gives
the diaries a coastal voice and breaks their shared two-beat rhythm.

## 9. Expansion hooks

For next volumes only; the sheet cannot carry any of it.

- **The lake town, before.** A prequel under lago-campanario: its keeper, its compuerta, the ajolote grown.
- **After *Bajas con él*.** The copying reaches land: hospital-respira, calle-ausente, the dock learned (nudo).
- **Chela's bill.** The sea has charged everyone but her. The day it does is a Capítulo in itself.
